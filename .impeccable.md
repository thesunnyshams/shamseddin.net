## Design Context

### Users

The primary readers are research faculty and technical internship screeners deciding quickly
whether Shamseddin does serious, credible work. Academic readers, collaborators, and peers are a
secondary audience. The site should give each reader a clear account of what he builds, why he
builds it, and what evidence supports the claims.

### Brand Personality

Rigorous, curious, humane. The voice is direct and personally written, with intellectual conviction
but no inflated claims. It should feel clear-eyed about uncertainty while remaining ambitious about
science and public benefit.

### Aesthetic Direction

An editorial scientific register built from the visual grammar of measurement. The composition is
left-weighted and typographic, with hairlines, a narrow evidence rail, square corners, self-hosted
fonts, and light and dark themes. Avoid generic portfolio cards, decorative scientific imagery,
gradients, glow, rounded surfaces, shadows, and anything that resembles a generated technology
template.

### Design Principles

1. Make evidence legible: numbers, dates, sources, and uncertainty should be easy to inspect.
2. Let conviction live in the writing, while the interface stays restrained and precise.
3. Preserve the hierarchy of a technical report instead of turning every item into a card.
4. Keep the site usable without JavaScript and comfortable with reduced motion.
5. Write for a smart reader who has little time: specific, concise, human, and never promotional.
