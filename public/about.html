About

Originally from Syria, I grew up in Turkey and studied for two years at Pearson College UWC on Vancouver Island, Canada. I am now double-majoring in Physics and Computer Science at St. Lawrence University as a Davis Scholar.

Using AI for Science

Much of AI's capability is being directed toward work that does not help humanity progress scientifically, and can sometimes set us back morally. I believe the physical sciences should receive far more of that capability. This is why I believe so strongly in using AI for science, and why computational physics is my passion.

Forecasting, catalogs, instruments, and data analysis are the kind of work that can add to humanity's shared wealth of knowledge. The knowledge and technologies that follow must be used for the benefit of everyone.

This is why I build what I build, and it is what I want to keep doing.

My work as a freelance AI Training Analyst taught me exactly where these models and systems fail. I design adversarial test cases and evaluate outputs for frontier labs. That work has left me permanently suspicious of confident answers, including my own, and clear-eyed about the limits of these systems.

Pearson College UWC

I spent two years at Pearson College UWC in Victoria, British Columbia, from August 2024 to May 2026. Living and working with students from different political, social, and economic backgrounds across more than 90 countries taught me to work with anyone. It also taught me that the best ideas usually come from outside the room where they are needed, whether across borders, specialties, or fields.

Most of what I started there came from deep curiosity: Pearson's first IA Fair, created to formalize the transfer of research advice from second-years to first-years and make that knowledge accessible to everyone; Global Lunches, a weekly forum created so the college's international diversity would remain something we actively learned from all year, not only something we noticed during orientation; and a Science Club created to strengthen the school's scientific culture and deepen its engagement with the local community.
