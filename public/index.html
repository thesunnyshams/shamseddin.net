Things I have built

Bowcast

Live · Source · Since June 2026. Web, with iOS and Android in progress.

Atmospheric physics·Software

A worldwide rainbow forecast. It works out where the sun is from your coordinates and the time, then checks whether rain is falling in the part of the sky where a rainbow would have to appear.

The number it shows is a weighted probability estimated from about 40 weather model runs, not a score I invented. When that ensemble is unavailable it says so rather than filling in a worse number in the same place.

Black Hole Census

Live · Source · Since July 2026. 3D field guide and logarithmic census.

Astronomy·Data engineering

Black hole populations are split across incompatible archives. Merger detections live in GWOSC, catalog cross-matches in VizieR, identifiers in SIMBAD, and extragalactic objects in NED. The Census brings those records into one field guide without flattening their different kinds of evidence.

Every measurement keeps its uncertainty and source. The interface shows how invisible objects are inferred through stellar motion, X-rays, horizon-scale imaging, and gravitational waves.

Pearson's climate action plan

September 2024 to May 2025. Submitted to the college board, which decides in early 2027.

Climate policy·Emissions accounting

The college's position on climate was assumed rather than written down. I co-drafted the plan that states it: a greenhouse gas baseline for the college's operations, measurable reduction targets across transport, food, buildings and waste, and the actions needed to meet them. It also sets out what stands in the way, including student turnover and the fact that nobody involved has much spare time.

It is not public until the board decides, so its figures are not on this page.
